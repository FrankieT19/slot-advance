# slot. advance v1.0

slot.'s cartridge frontend for EZ-FLASH Omega and original Omega DE, built on DS Style.

Download the ZIP for your cartridge. Copy the contents of **SD_CARD** to your card, then hold **R** while powering on to update.

Each ZIP includes **Label Converter.exe**, an empty **USERNAME.TXT**, and a short readme. Scrape artwork with [slot. Cart Studio](https://studio.slot-cfw.fyi/), then run the converter on your PC.

[Installation and controls](https://github.com/FrankieT19/slot-advance#install). The newer DE B / EZODE-B is not supported.
